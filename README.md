# Chocolate Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive and dynamic Power BI Dashboard built to analyze global chocolate sales performance data (containing ~22,000 transactions). This project provides valuable insights into revenue distribution, product popularity, marketing spend efficiency, channel performance, and salesperson productivity.

The visual layout of the completed project can be referenced from the workspace screenshot saved as Chocolate_sales_dashboard.JPEG

---

## 📊 Dashboard Key Features & Layout

### 1. High-Level KPI Summary (Top-Left Cards)
*   Total Sales Amount / Revenue: Highlights the overall gross revenue generated (Totaling $101.82M across the entire dataset period).
*   Total Boxes Shipped: Tracks the physical volume sold, reaching 28M boxes.
*   Average Discount %: Shows the global average markdown applied per box (13.11%).
*   Total Unique Orders: Displays the total volume of independent incoming transactions (199K orders).

### 2. Marketing Spend vs Revenue (Scatter Plot)
*   Sum of Marketing Spend by Amount: Maps out marketing expenses directly against the revenue generated per order to evaluate advertising effectiveness and ROI.

### 3. Sales Breakdown by Channel (Donut Chart)
*   Visualizes market share split across three core distribution channels:
    *   Retail: The largest driver, capturing $53.38M (52.42%) of total sales.
    *   Wholesale: Accounting for $37.23M (36.56%).
    *   Online: Driving $11.21M (11.01%).

### 4. Product Demand Analysis (Horizontal Bar Chart)
*   Sum of Amount by Product: Rankings of the top chocolate offerings. High performers like the 70% Dark Bar, Mixed Assortment, and Truffle Gift Box lead the chart.

### 5. Sales Performance Trend Over Time (Line Chart)
*   Sum of Amount by Order_Date: Tracks the macroeconomic performance flow continuously from January 2022 through December 2023, uncovering cyclical seasonal sales patterns and peak order intervals.

### 6. Team & Regional Performance Tracking
*   Salesperson Matrix Leaderboard: A tabular ranking showcasing top revenue generators (e.g., *Arjun Mehta* leading significantly with $34.45M, *Emily Clarke* at $9.05M).
*   Interactive Slicers: Full-page cross-filtering controls for Total Sales Period (Date Range), Country (*Australia, Brazil, Germany, India, Japan*), and Sales Type/Channel (*Online, Retail, Wholesale*).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
*   Power BI Desktop – Core data ingestion, relationship modeling, DAX measure creation, and report interface design.
*   Power Query – Data cleaning, fixing character encoding issues (e.g., correcting names like Hannah Müller), type casting, and dataset profiling.
*   GitHub – Version control and portfolio hosting.

---

## 📈 Key Insights Extracted
1.  Core Revenue Driver: The *Retail* channel contributes more than half of the entire global business revenue ($53.38M), making it the primary strategic growth pillar.
2.  Top Performer Impact: A small segment of the sales force drives a disproportionately massive share of the business—specifically *Arjun Mehta*, who individually accounted for over $34.4M of the $101.8M total sales.
3.  Product Leader: The 70% Dark Bar stands out as the ultimate flagship product, commanding the highest cumulative revenue share among all stock units.
